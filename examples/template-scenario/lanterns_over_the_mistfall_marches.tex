\textit{For nine generations the }\textbf{\textit{Lantern Keepers}}\textit{ have tended the beacons of the Mistfall Marches. Tonight the fog rolls in from the fens, the lamps gutter one by one, and something with far too many eyes is walking the old road. The last Keeper's order was simple: ``Keep the flame -- whatever comes.'' Light the Beacon, or the Marches go dark for good.}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Scenario Length}}

12 Rounds (+2 Rounds once the \textbf{Beacon} is lit)

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Player Setup}}

\textbf{Player Count:} 1 or 2

\textbf{Faction:} Rampart

\textbf{Faction Hero:} Gem, the \textit{Lantern Keeper}

\textbf{Starting Resources:} 12 \svg{gold}, 3 \svg{building_materials}, 1 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Income:} 15 \svg{gold}, 2 \svg{building_materials}, 0 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Units:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A ``Pack'' of the cheapest \svg{bronze} Units
  \item A ``Few'' of the cheapest \svg{silver} Units
  \item \textbf{One} \svg{golden} Unit of your choice -- \textit{only} if your Hero holds the ``Watchtower''
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Town Buildings:} \svg{bronze} Dwelling, \svg{silver} Dwelling, City Hall, Mage Guild, \svg{building_special_tent} Building

\textbf{Map Tile Pool:} Each player takes 2 random Far (II--III) Map Tiles and 1 random Near (IV--V) Map Tile. These Tiles should not contain any Obelisks.

\textbf{Bonus:} Choose one of the following options:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Search (2) the Artifact Deck
  \item Roll a Treasure Die
  \item +1 \svg{experience}
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{AI Hero Setup}}

\textbf{Faction:} Necropolis

\textbf{Enemy Army:} 2 \svg{bronze}, 2 \svg{silver}, 1 \svg{golden} \textbf{Necropolis} Units

\textbf{Enemy Deck:} Might AI Deck, Normal

\textbf{Enemy Spell Deck:} None

\textbf{Enemy Skills:} Offense, Armorer

\textbf{Special:} The AI Hero \textit{never} retreats and ignores the Fog rules below.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Map Setup}}

Take the following Map Tiles and arrange them as shown in the Scenario map layout:

\textbf{For 1 player:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item 1 × Starting (I) Map Tile with the \textbf{Faction} border facing the Far Tiles
  \item 2 × Far (II--III) Map Tiles one of them with the Pyramid Field
  \item 1 × Near (IV--V) Map Tile with the Gate Field
  \item 1 × Center (VI--VII) Map Tile (the \textit{Beacon} Tile, with a Black Cube on its Monolith Field)
  \item 1 × Water Map Tile
\end{itemize}

\textbf{For 2 players:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item 2 × Starting (I) Map Tiles with the \textbf{Faction} border facing the Far Tiles
  \item 2 × Far (II--III) Map Tiles one of them with the Pyramid Field
  \item 2 × Near (IV--V) Map Tiles with the Gate Field
  \item 1 × Center (VI--VII) Map Tile (the \textit{Beacon} Tile, with a Black Cube on its Monolith Field)
  \item 1 × Water Map Tile
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Keep the remaining Map Tiles in a \textit{separate} face-down pile.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Heroes Placement}}

Place your Main Hero on the Starting Tile with the Rampart border. In a 2-player game the second Hero starts on the Starting Tile with the Castle border.

The AI Hero starts on the printed Far Tile, on the Field with the Necropolis Cube.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Conditions}}

Light the Beacon: your Main Hero must stand on the Monolith Field of the Center Tile and spend 2 \svg{movement} there. Lighting the Beacon ends the Scenario in victory.

Optional goal: \textbf{defeat} the AI Hero first to earn the \textit{Lantern Banner} bonus.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Defeat Conditions}}

Your Main Hero is defeated in battle (retreat does not count).

The Beacon is still dark after the last Round.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Timed Events}}

\textbf{\nth{3} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Each player gains 1 \svg{experience}.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\nth{5} and \nth{8} Rounds:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Spawn an enemy army following the rules below.
  \item All Heroes lose 1 \svg{movement} this Round -- the \textbf{fog} is thick.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\nth{11} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Repeat the Timed Events of Rounds 5 and 8.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Additional Rules}}

During this Scenario, the following rules apply:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Neutral Units do not respawn.
  \item Players cannot attack the Heroes of other players.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{The Fog}}

The fog is a \textit{living thing}. While it lasts, no Hero can see farther than one Field.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Heroes standing next to a lit Lantern ignore this rule.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Enemy Spawning and Movement}}

Enemy armies come out of the fog on the \textbf{Fog Fields} at the edge of the map, one army per spawn.
\begin{itemize}
  \item The army is made of 2 \svg{bronze} and 1 \svg{silver} \textbf{Necropolis} Units, placed on the Fog Field nearest to the AI Hero.
  \item At the end of each Round, every enemy army moves up to 2 Fields toward the nearest Hero. An army that reaches a Hero starts a battle.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{The Story}}

The Keepers never asked for thanks, only for oil and a little quiet. When the Valley Council cut their pay of 3 \svg{gold} a season, they kept the lamps lit anyway.

Then the fog came. It did not roll like weather; it walked. By the third night the farms along the Lantern Road were silent, and by the fifth the first beacon went dark. The Keeper at Greywatch sent one last rider south with a single message: the \textbf{Beacon} on Mistfall Hill must burn, and only a Hero of the Marches can reach it.

You are that Hero. Behind you the road is already vanishing. Ahead, the old lamps are waiting for a hand steady enough to relight them.

\end{multicols*}
