\textit{For nine generations the }\textbf{\textit{Lantern Keepers}}\textit{ of Greywatch have tended the lamps of the Mistfall Marches. Now a fog that walks has crept out of the fens, the Lanterns of the old road have gone dark, and the dead are moving inside it.}

\textit{Relight the Lanterns, climb Mistfall Hill and light the great Beacon -- before the Marches go dark for good.}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Scenario Length}}

12 Rounds (+2 Rounds once the \textbf{Beacon} is lit)

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Player Setup}}

\textbf{Player Count:} 1 or 2

\textbf{Faction:} Rampart (in a 2-player game, the second player plays Castle)

\textbf{Faction Hero:} Gem, the \textit{Lantern Keeper} (the second player takes any Castle Hero)

\textbf{Starting Resources:} 10 \svg{gold}, 3 \svg{building_materials}, 1 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Income:} 10 \svg{gold}, 0 \svg{building_materials}, 0 \svg{valuables}

\textbf{Starting Units:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A ``Few'' Centaurs and a ``Few'' Dwarves
  \item \textit{2-player game:} the second player takes a ``Few'' Halberdiers and a ``Few'' Marksmen
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Town Buildings:} \svg{bronze} Dwelling, Citadel

\textbf{Map Tile Pool:} Each player takes 1 random Far (II--III) Map Tile. You can place it only next to your own Starting Tile.

\textbf{Bonus:} Choose one of the following options:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Search (2) the Artifact Deck.
  \item Search (2) the Spell Deck.
  \item Roll two Resource Dice and gain one result.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{AI Hero Setup}}

\textbf{Faction:} Necropolis

\textbf{Enemy Army:} A ``Pack'' of Skeletons, a ``Pack'' of Wraiths, a ``Few'' Vampires, a ``Few'' Liches and a ``Few'' Dread Knights (\textit{2-player game:} her Vampires and Liches are ``Packs'')

\textbf{Enemy Deck:} 3 × Might Cards, 2 × Magic Cards, 1 × Skill Card

\textbf{Enemy Spell Deck:} 1 × Weakness, 1 × Curse, 1 × Lightning Bolt

\textbf{Enemy Skills:} Armorer

\textbf{Special:} The Mist Witch waits in the Barrow until the Beacon is lit (see \textit{The Mist Witch} below).

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Map Setup}}

Take the following Map Tiles and arrange them as shown in the Scenario map layout:

\textbf{For 1 player:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item 1 × Starting (I) Map Tile -- Rampart S4 (and Castle S3 in a 2-player game)
  \item 4 × Far (II--III) Map Tiles -- random, face down
  \item 3 × Near (IV--V) Map Tiles -- N3 and N4 (the Lanterns), face down, and N7 (the Beacon), face up
  \item 1 × Water Map Tile
  \item 1 × Subterranean Map Tile
\end{itemize}

\textbf{For 2 players:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item 2 × Starting (I) Map Tiles -- Rampart S4 (and Castle S3 in a 2-player game)
  \item 5 × Far (II--III) Map Tiles -- random, face down
  \item 3 × Near (IV--V) Map Tiles -- N3 and N4 (the Lanterns), face down, and N7 (the Beacon), face up
  \item 1 × Water Map Tile
  \item 1 × Subterranean Map Tile
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}
  \item You need the Rampart, Stronghold and Cove expansions.
  \item The Water Tile is W1 (the Mere), face down. The Subterranean Tile is U2 (the Barrow), face up beside N7; the map draws it faded because it lies underground.
  \item Place the Subterranean Gate between N7 and the Barrow as shown, and a Black Cube on the Beacon (the Obelisk of N7).
  \item When you discover W1, place the Derelict Ship Creature Bank (Naval Battles) on its Blocked Field; without that expansion the Field stays Blocked.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Heroes Placement}}

Place Gem on the Town Field of S4 (Greywatch). In a 2-player game, the second player places their Main Hero on the Town Field of S3.

Place a Necropolis Hero model for the Mist Witch on the Witch Hut Field of the Barrow (U2).

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Victory Conditions}}

Light the Beacon on Mistfall Hill and defeat the Mist Witch, in either order.

When you have done both, read ``Dawn over the Marches'' in The Story.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Defeat Conditions}}

Gem, the Rampart Main Hero, loses a Combat (Retreating from Neutral Units does not count).

The Scenario ends before you win: after the 12th Round, or after the 14th once the Beacon is lit.

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Timed Events}}

\textbf{\nth{1} Round:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Read ``The Last Rider'' in The Story.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{\nth{5}, \nth{8} and \nth{11} Rounds:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A Fog Army rises out of the Barrow onto the Subterranean Gate: Level III in the 5th Round, IV in the 8th and V in the 11th (one Level higher in a 2-player game). See \textit{Fog Armies}.
  \item Skip this event once the Mist Witch is defeated.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Additional Rules}}

During this Scenario, the following rules apply:
\begin{itemize}
  \item You cannot recruit a Secondary Hero.
  \item Main Heroes cannot advance past Level V.
  \item Astrologers Proclaim Cards are not used -- the fog hides the stars.
  \item \textit{2-player game:} the players are allies. They cannot attack each other or take each other's flagged Fields, and they can trade Resources and Artifact Cards while their Heroes stand on adjacent Fields.
  \item \textit{2-player game:} if the second player's Main Hero loses a Combat, move them to their Town; that player loses 5 \svg{gold} and both players gain \svg{morale_negative}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{The Mistfall}}

Every Map Tile lies in the fog, except each Town's Tile and the Tiles next to it, and each lit Lantern's Tile and the Tiles next to it (see \textit{Lanterns and the Beacon}).
\begin{itemize}
  \item In the fog, every guarded Field is one Level higher: use the next row of the Field Difficulty Level Table for its Neutral Units, Quick Combat and Experience (a Level VII Field stays VII).
  \item The red crosses on the map are Mist Walls: Blocked Fields for as long as they lie in the fog. They seal Mistfall Hill until a Lantern clears a way.
  \item The fog does not hinder the Mist Witch and the Fog Armies.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Lanterns and the Beacon}}

The Obelisks of N3 and N4 are the Lanterns of the Lantern Road; the Obelisk of N7 is the Beacon. A Black Cube on an Obelisk means it is dark: the Beacon has one from the start, and each Lantern gets one when you discover its Tile.
\begin{itemize}
  \item To light a Lantern or the Beacon, flag its Obelisk: replace the Black Cube with your Faction Cube. It never goes out again.
  \item When you light a Lantern, gain 1 \svg{experience} and discover every face-down Map Tile next to its Tile for free. The first time, read ``A Lantern Rekindled''.
  \item When you light the Beacon, the fog lifts from the whole map and the Scenario lasts 2 more Rounds. Read ``The Beacon Burns''.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{Fog Armies}}

Before the game, take the Necropolis Neutral Units (Skeletons, Zombies, Wraiths, Vampires, Liches, Dread Knights and Ghost Dragons) out of the Neutral Unit Decks: they form the Mist Deck.
\begin{itemize}
  \item A Fog Army appears on the Subterranean Gate (see Timed Events). Draw its Units from the Mist Deck as for a Field of its Level on the Field Difficulty Level Table (from the regular Neutral Unit Deck if a tier runs out) and mark it with a Necropolis Cube. If a Hero stands there, they fight at once.
  \item At the end of each Round, every Fog Army moves 2 Fields toward the closest Hero (the players choose on a tie). It does not flag or visit Fields, crosses face-down Tiles without discovering them and may pass Blocked Fields, but never stops on one.
  \item When a Fog Army reaches a Hero, or a Hero enters its Field, they fight. For Experience it counts as Neutral Units guarding a Field of its Level, but there is no Quick Combat, no time limit and no Retreat.
  \item When you defeat a Fog Army, gain 3 \svg{gold} and shuffle its Units back into the Mist Deck.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{The Mist Witch}}

The Mist Witch is the AI Hero. Until the Beacon is lit she does not move, even when a Hero enters the Barrow, which can only be reached through the Subterranean Gate on N7.
\begin{itemize}
  \item You can go down and attack her at any time. Once she is defeated, no more Fog Armies appear.
  \item At the end of the Round in which the Beacon is lit, she rises: place her on the Subterranean Gate (if a Hero stands there, she attacks at once). From then on, after the players' Turns, she moves up to 3 Fields toward the closest Main Hero the way Fog Armies move, and attacks when she reaches them.
  \item Combat with her follows the AI rules: no time limit, no Retreat and no surrender.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{\MakeUppercase{The Story}}

\textbf{The Last Rider}

The Keepers never asked for thanks, only for oil and a little quiet. When the Valley Council cut their pay of 3 \svg{gold} a season, they kept the lamps lit anyway.

Then the fog came. It did not roll like weather; it walked. By the third night the farms along the Lantern Road were silent, and by the fifth the last lamp on the road went dark. The old Keeper of Greywatch sent out one rider with a single message: ``Light the Lanterns of the road, then the \textbf{Beacon} on Mistfall Hill. And do not go down into the Barrow before you are ready.''

\textbf{A Lantern Rekindled}

The wick takes the flame on the third try. Light spills across the marsh, and the fog draws back like a hand from a hot stove. Far to the east, under the hill, something screams.

\textbf{The Beacon Burns}

The Beacon roars into life, and the fog tears apart like wet paper. If the Mist Witch still lives, the hill shudders under your feet: the Barrow gate grinds open, and she climbs into the light with her dead around her, to put out the flame -- and you with it.

\textbf{Dawn over the Marches}

The Witch falls, and the last of the fog sinks into the fens like water into sand. One by one, the Lanterns of the old road answer the Beacon.

Before she fell, the Witch spoke a name. It was not her own. Keep your Units and Resources for the next Scenario of the Campaign.
